HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. One codebase.

From a one-paragraph brief to a merged, working project — your whole team building at once.

1 Decompose

Mosaic AI reads your brief and splits it into 3–5 parallelizable tasks, each with explicit interface contracts so nothing collides later.

2 Build in parallel

Each teammate gets a Monaco editor and a private AI session that knows their files plus every shared contract. See who's coding, done, or blocked live.

3 Semantic merge
✓

When everyone submits, Mosaic reviews all branches and resolves integration conflicts by meaning — not just lines — into one project you download as a ZIP.
